# Sponsoring

<a id="sponsoring"></a>

Thanks for considering it. Sponsorship keeps the free catalog maintained.

| Tier | Monthly | What you get |
|---|---:|---|
| Supporter | $5 | Sponsor badge |
| Team | $150 | Name in SPONSORS.md |
| Business | $500, 3-month minimum | Labeled logo in the README and docs |
| Featured | $1,500, 3-month minimum, one slot | Top labeled logo and a line in release notes |

All placements are marked as sponsored. Sponsorship never buys a listing in the catalog or a change to a scorecard. Payment is through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/wshobson).
